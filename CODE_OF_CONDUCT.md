# Code of Conduct

Help make ULPG a welcoming place to learn and contribute. Treat people respectfully, discuss ideas with evidence, credit others' work, and make room for different abilities, backgrounds, and levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, disclosure of private information, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or close discussions to protect contributors. Responses should be proportionate, explain the relevant behavior where appropriate, and protect the privacy of affected people. Maintainers are held to the same expectations.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for private abuse reports, or a maintainer contact explicitly published on the repository owner's profile. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an issue. Until a dedicated private contact is published, this project does not offer its own confidential reporting channel.
